Score formula

Quality score
100 − missing penalty
− duplicate penalty
− structural penalties

Missing and duplicate deductions scale with their share of cells and rows. Structural deductions scale with affected rows, with a minimum weight per triggered rule and a 40-point cap.

Limits

  • Rules check structural and heuristic patterns; they do not prove semantic correctness.
  • Business importance is not inferred; all columns are assessed by affected-row share.
  • Sensitive-data detection is heuristic and can miss personal data.
  • Masking and pseudonymization are not legal anonymization guarantees.
