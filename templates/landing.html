Workflow

From raw file to a version you can explain.

Every step is explicit. Preview changes before they are written, and keep a trace of what was applied.

01

Upload

Choose a CSV or XLSX file. DataSieve detects the format, delimiter, encoding, and workbook sheets.

02

Profile

Inspect row and column counts, types, missing values, uniqueness, distributions, and inferred patterns.

03

Review findings

Work through rule-level findings with severity, affected rows, explanations, and recommended next steps.

04

Clean & export

Preview a cleaning operation, apply it to a new version, then export the data or a quality report.

Quality checks

Transparent rules, not a black-box score.

The score is traceable to the findings that reduced it. Potential outliers are reported without silently penalising the score.

Ten structural checks

Missing values, duplicates, duplicate headers, whitespace, mixed types, category variants, date formats, outliers, sensitive patterns, and constant columns.

Versioned cleaning

Each cleaning job previews row and cell impact first. Applied changes become a new version with the source left intact.

Privacy guardrails

Column values are redacted in previews when names or patterns suggest emails, phones, identifiers, or other personal data.

Read the rulebook before the first upload.

Definitions, severities, score weights, and limitations are published in the app.
